To: info@kenyalaw.org

Subject: Authorized legislation and judgment-reference access for Sheria Check

Dear Kenya Law team,

I am developing Sheria Check, an online Kenyan legal-information tool for web, Android and iOS. The initial product helps the public find original legislation and offense/penalty provisions, read rights references, and follow links to public judgments. It requires no motorist account and does not collect personal case histories or incident reports.

The interface states that it provides legal information, is not a lawyer and does not replace advice from a qualified advocate. We intend to preserve citations, source versions, attribution and published anonymization, and to review material before publication.

Please advise on an authorized API or bulk-data licensing route for this initial collection:

- Constitution of Kenya, Traffic Act and selected traffic regulations;
- Penal Code, Criminal Procedure Code, Evidence Act and Legal Aid Act;
- selected public judgment citations, court/date metadata and official source links relevant to those provisions.

We would appreciate clarification on:

1. Permitted collection, local indexing, in-app display and redistribution of original unannotated legal text, separately from publisher annotations/headnotes or document layout.
2. Access methods, authentication, rate limits, fees, attribution requirements and any noncommercial/commercial-use restrictions.
3. Version histories, amendment/commencement metadata, correction notices and withdrawal/anonymization updates we should support.
4. Conditions for displaying judgment-reference metadata and linking to original public decisions without reproducing private or restricted records.
5. Any additional permission that would be needed for a later, separately reviewed source-grounded AI explanation feature. AI and case summaries are currently deferred; this request does not assume permission for model training or for sending personal data to an AI provider.

We have paused automated corpus acquisition and publication while confirming a permitted route. Please let us know the application process, required project details and suitable point of contact.
